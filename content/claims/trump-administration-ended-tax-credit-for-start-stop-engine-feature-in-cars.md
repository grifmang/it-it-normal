summary: >-
  The Trump administration eliminated regulatory credit that automakers received
  under federal vehicle emissions standards for automatic start-stop engine
  technology, effective for model year 2027 vehicles. This was not a tax credit
  for consumers but rather a credit manufacturers used to comply with Corporate
  Average Fuel Economy (CAFE) standards and greenhouse gas emissions
  requirements.
