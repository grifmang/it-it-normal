summary: >-
  The term 'hothouse Earth' refers to a scientific hypothesis about potential
  climate tipping points, not an imminent death spiral. While scientists have
  documented concerning warming trends and the crossing of some planetary
  boundaries, the claim that Earth is on track to become uninhabitable
  represents an exaggeration of current scientific consensus, which indicates
  serious risks but not near-term uninhabitability.
