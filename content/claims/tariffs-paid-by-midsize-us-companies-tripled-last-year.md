summary: >-
  According to a JPMorganChase Institute study released in February 2025, tariff
  payments by midsize US companies increased approximately threefold in 2024
  compared to 2023. The study analyzed transaction data from companies with
  annual revenues between $20 million and $5 billion and found tariff payments
  rose from roughly 1% of goods value to approximately 3% during this period.
